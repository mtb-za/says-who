A minor rework in the tasks was the main change this session.
Currently the PCs have the following tasks on their lists, with NPCs doing other things in the background.
Since most of the PCs are on the technical and support side, most of these are maintenance and improvement things, more than actually doing research work.
These tasks are larger scale things than previously, and we are working in time units of a week, so there is time to support other tasks as well.

Anastasia Volkova has brought the two security people (Jan Pettersson [PC] and Peter Chemerkin [NPC]) into the existence of a potential saboteur on the base.
They are to do a _very_ quiet investigation into what might be going on.

Another big development: NIGHT HAS FALLEN.
We have a couple of weeks (two sessions) of lunar night to look forward to, making exterior operations more risky.

The interplay of solving problems over a week is quite satisfying, since you can just step over to another PC and help out or ask for some help in most cases.
Focusing the tasks has also been useful, since it does not feel like we are the only ones needing to handle things on the base.
The implication is of course that NPCs are taking care of other issues in the background, although we are running into them quite often in various ways.

Things are largely focusing in Stankevičius, Volkova, Chemerkin, and Fritz as the most prominent NPCs, who are all interesting and have their own thing going on.

The troubles continue to drive things forward: Agramonte is haunted by past failures and does not want to repeat them, the Cold War has some more stuff in the background ramping up but that is still off in the distance, Wagner had a pretty major crashout as a result of her Trouble which is pretty compelling.
This is definitely moving the campaign along in a nice way.

## Underground Base Construction - Teresa Wagner

Long-term plan is to have underground section for additional shielding from cosmic rays and things and moves are finally being made to actually get this done.
Need to pick the specific site within the Plato Crater and do some testing and blasting of those sites.
Still needs outside work, but within safety distance, so it will "probably be definitely fine" in the case of something going wrong.

Taking MOLAB, none are ideal: one has life support issue, one has bearing issue that has not been replaced.
Is going out alone - technically against the regs, but is close enough to walk home, so it will be fine.

Has a few options of places to check out, will start with furtherest away:
- **Crater Wall**:
  Takes some samples to get an idea of construction suitability.
  This is difficult because of shadows and similar things making visibility tricky.
  Also tricky to do alone, but there is a latch missing, so assembly can not be completed.
  - Need to find that piece to see if just dropped or in MOLAB or what.
    Does a bodged temporary fix instead, gets samples and then looks properly for the fastener; seems to have rolled off somewhere and disappeared (for now).
- Other two locations should be more straightforward.
  The jury-rigged part might get damaged during drive, but will risk it, successfully.
  - Has gotten all three sites sampled.

Comes back to a lab that has been attacked by a label-maker, although things are still not organised correctly.

Of the sites:
- The geology under the base looks to be adequate, but will potentially be impacted by the base itself.
  Very convenient, but mistakes could have major repercussions.
- The middle area is weirdly not-dense: easy to excavate, but will need additional support for larger structures - needs to be smaller or will be more expensive
- The wall itself is easier to dig, but the geology is much more varied, so construction is more risky and complicated.
  This is the best option if safety can be overcome, but is also further from the base.
