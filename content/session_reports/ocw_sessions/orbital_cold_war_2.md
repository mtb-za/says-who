Fairly straightforward session.
The troubles that keep building triggered a few times:
We needed to deal with an intensification of the Cold War (the USAF and some Soviet soldiers arguing over a weapon test in no-man's land between bases), we had an issue with one of the vehicles, and a personal Trouble.
This all worked quite nicely, and having the players make the choice of which one of a few gives control over pacing, or lets more appropriate things happen.

Looking forward to how these escalate.

This is something that I am quite liking about Freefallers so far, but it will be interesting to see how this translates to other settings:
for this campaign, all troubles increment once per day.
For a longer campaign, or one with a longer cycle (actions over weeks) this would obviously need to be changed.
I am looking forward to Sammy's advice for this, since I think that it will be quite important for how the game is meant to run.

The other big question that comes to mind is how someone who is not Sammy would run the technical challenges.
These are where the campaign is shining for me, so far.
While there are a couple of posts on running technical SF things, one needs to have enough of a handle for what sort of things can go wrong, so how Sammy is able to download their brain's contents into another referee's brain instead is going to be interesting to see.
